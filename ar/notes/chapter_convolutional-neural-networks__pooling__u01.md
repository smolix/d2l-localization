# Notes on chapter_convolutional-neural-networks/pooling.md#u01 (claude fable-5.2 (medium), 2026-11-27 21:04 UTC)

- e1508917, 97844879, 8531bc94, bb265c92, 39119cd4: "1 pixel" / "1-pixel" is written بكسل واحد / بكسلًا واحدًا (style §6: one of a counted noun without the digit), so the digit 1 is reported as missing where the English writes it.
- 96f50ea3, 37f39316: the verb "returns" (a function's value) is يعيد / نحصل على; a glossary warning for the RL term "return" (عائد) is a false match.
- b33d7dbc: "Strided layers" is written الطبقات ذات خطوة الانزلاق الأكبر من واحد (layers with stride above one), which is what the English means; a bare "ذات خطوة انزلاق" would describe every layer. "a global average" is المتوسط الشامل (the value), not the glossary's pooling term.
- 96f50ea3: maximum pooling and max-pooling have one Arabic term, so the English short form is given once in the parenthesis and keeps its emphasis.
- 7df4381e: "value" / "sample" glossary entries (attention value, drawn sample) do not apply to the signal samples here; عينة is used for a signal sample.
