## 2026-11-27: author rulings applied

The author ruled on `ar/style.md` §12–§13 on 2026-11-27 (now `ar/style.md` §12, "Decisions"), after three evidence reports: the two earlier Arabic D2L editions, Arabic ML writing on the web with the SDAIA/ICAIRE glossary as the primary reference, and the prose of SDAIA and MBZUAI publications.
